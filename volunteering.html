What
The Women in Engineering (WiE) committee at the University of Waterloo gathers members from a variety of backgrounds focused on inspiring female-identifying students to thrive in their future careers. The committee provides resources, programs, and networking opportinities for women in engineering at Waterloo, ON. I have been a member of the WiE committee for two years, period in which I helped organizing activities such as panel discussions with WiE leaders in industry and academia, as well as career fairs. To find out more about the program, visit their website or their social media page.

What
United for Literacy is an organization that delivers literacy programs to people in communities across Canada. I joined the program during the summer of 2023 as a math tutor in Number Ninjas, an interactive numeracy program for elementary learners in grades 1-3. The program took place at the Waterloo Public Library. To find find out more about the program, visit their website.

Where
Program Incluir

Role
English teacher and coordinator

When
Aug/2019 - Mar/2022

What
The Incluir program is tied to the Federal University of Minas Gerais (Brazil) and promotes social inclusion through education, oferring courses to support personal, academic, and professional development of people in underserved communities. I was part of the project during two and a half years, first as an English teacher and later as a coordinator as well. I taught weekly classes to children from 7 to 12 years old. To find out more about the program, visit their social media page (content in portuguese).
