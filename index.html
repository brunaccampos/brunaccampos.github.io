Bruna Campos

I am a civil engineer specializing in computational mechanics, finite element modelling, and scientific software development. My research focuses on computational modelling of multiphysics systems, including fluid flow, porous media, and wave propagation.

My current work as a postdoctoral fellow at Polytechnique Montreal involves the development of open-source CFD software for rotating reactors, and the simulation of passive particle transport within a fluid using population balance equations.

I was born in Brazil and moved to Canada to start my PhD. I have also lived in London, UK during a one-year academic exchange program. My "saved places" list on Google Maps is full with all the cities I have visited, and I am always looking forward to explore a new place. Besides traveling, I enjoy reading, exercising, spending time with family and friends, and a bit of coding for fun.
